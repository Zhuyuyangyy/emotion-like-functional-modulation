## 1. Overview

This document provides a transparent account of the origin, nature, and authenticity of all datasets used in this study. No real-world deployment data, production logs, or user-identifiable information is included.

---

## 2. Affective-Agent-Safety-300 (Semi-Real-300)

- **Nature**: Semi-real / structured simulated benchmark
- **Origin**: Constructed from structured scenario templates designed to reflect realistic agent safety decision points
- **Sample size**: 300 cases
- **Categories**: coding_agent_trace (100), tool_use_risk_trace (80), affective_pressure_trace (60), safe_low_risk_trace (40), experience_failure_trace (20)
- **Label source**: Structured heuristic mapping from risk level to safety decision (CRITICAL→BLOCK, HIGH→HUMAN_REVIEW, MEDIUM→SIMULATE_FIRST, LOW→AUTO_EXECUTE)
- **Real-world deployment evidence**: None
- **Production / real-world deployment traces**: None
- **Claim of real user logs**: No

### Important Caveat
The original Semi-Real-300 source JSON file was not found in the current repository. The main results reported in the paper are based on this benchmark as described in prior work. The regenerated AffectiveBenchmark-300 (see Section 3 below) is a separate dataset and must not be conflated with Semi-Real-300.

---

## 3. AffectiveBenchmark-300 (Regenerated LLM Stress Test Set)

- **Nature**: Controlled benchmark, fully synthetic
- **Origin**: Generated by `AffectiveBenchmark(seed=42, size=300)` in `emotion_agent/affective_benchmark.py`
- **Sample size**: 300 cases
- **Categories**: irreversible_file_ops (51), trust_source_advice (65), high_uncertainty (62), high_reward_risk (68), recovery_generalization (54)
- **Label source**: Deterministic heuristic mapping from risk level to safety decision
- **Relationship to Semi-Real-300**: NOT the same benchmark. Different category names, different distribution, different schema. Used exclusively as an auxiliary stress test for the DeepSeek-v4-flash LLM safety judge.
- **Cannot be mixed into the main comparison table**: Correct — it appears only in a separate supplementary table.

## 4. AffectiveBench-100 (Original Controlled Benchmark)

- **Nature**: Controlled benchmark, fully synthetic
- **Origin**: Generated by `AffectiveBenchmark(seed=42, size=100)` in `emotion_agent/affective_benchmark.py`
- **Sample size**: 100 cases
- **Categories**: 5 categories, 20 tasks each
- **Label source**: Deterministic heuristic mapping
- **Use**: Annotation reliability study (100-case blind annotation sample)

## 6. Data Integrity

- All benchmark generation uses fixed random seeds (seed=42) for reproducibility
- No data augmentation or post-hoc label adjustment was performed on the reported results
- The DeepSeek-v4-flash stress test was executed with temperature=0.0 for deterministic output
- Raw outputs, predictions, and metrics are saved in `experiments/results/llm_baseline/`

---

## 7. Ethical Notes

- No human subjects were involved in data collection
- No personally identifiable information is present in any dataset
- The annotation protocol has been prepared for future human annotation but has not yet been executed
- All datasets are synthetic or structured simulations

---

## 8. Summary

This study relies entirely on controlled and semi-real simulated benchmarks. No real-world deployment data is claimed or implied. The AffectiveBenchmark-300 LLM stress test is clearly separated from the main Semi-Real-300 benchmark and must not be conflated.
