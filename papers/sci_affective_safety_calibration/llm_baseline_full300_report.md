## Key Findings
Under the tested zero-shot prompt and DeepSeek-v4-flash setting, the LLM safety judge exhibited extreme over-escalation:
1. **Perfect risk avoidance**: 0% risky auto-execution rate (no unsafe operations auto-executed)
2. **Severe operational paralysis**: 92.35% false caution rate (most safe operations escalated)
3. **Low auto-execution coverage**: Only 7.65% of safe operations auto-executed
4. **No middle ground**: Zero SIMULATE_FIRST predictions — only extremes (HUMAN_REVIEW/BLOCK/AUTO_EXECUTE)
5. **Full-300 confirms 100-case trend**: The pattern holds across both sample sizes

## Interpretation
These results highlight a key trade-off in safety systems:
- **Keyword baselines**: Too permissive (high risky auto-execution, low false caution)
- **Zero-shot LLM safety judges**: Too restrictive (low risky auto-execution, high false caution)
- **FullCalibratorAdapter**: Balances both (moderate risky auto-execution, moderate false caution)
